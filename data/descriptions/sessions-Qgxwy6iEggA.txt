Most people don't ignore their bank balance because they're bad with money, they ignore it because money is emotional. In this Keystone Sessions talk, Miriah breaks down why financial stress hits young adults transitioning to independence especially hard, and shares practical tools to build confidence one small decision at a time.

Miriah's work focuses on financial literacy for young people aging out of foster care, but the tools she shares, from decision-making frameworks to reframing financial setbacks, apply to anyone navigating money stress and building independence for the first time.
