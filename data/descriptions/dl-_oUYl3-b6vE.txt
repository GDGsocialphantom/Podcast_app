Learn about KC IMAGINE, a 501(c)(3) non-profit dedicated to expanding the digital media industry in Kansas City and beyond. By uniting digital media firms, educational institutions, career awareness programs, and civic groups, KC IMAGINE aims to develop a world-class industry supported by a strong talent pipeline and driven by innovative entrepreneurs. Discover how this organization is paving the way for a thriving digital media ecosystem and creating pathways for future talent and innovation in Kansas City.
