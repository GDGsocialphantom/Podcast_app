Phil Ledgerwood breaks down why most organizations get AI automation backwards — and why supercharging a broken process only makes the mess bigger, faster. In this Keystone Sessions talk, Phil walks through real client examples (including a biotech firm and an insurance company) to show where AI genuinely adds value, where it's dangerous, and why judgment still belongs to humans.

Key ideas covered:
✅ Why optimizing your process has to come before automating it
✅ The real difference between low-stakes and mission-critical AI use cases
✅ Why AI "can't think" — and what that means for decisions like underwriting
✅ How to identify the tasks only a human should own
✅ A simple test for figuring out what to hand off to AI (and what never to)
