What does it take to turn a city into a place Hollywood calls first?

For ten years, Paige Beltowski traveled with her sewing machines in her checked bags. She built costumes for Star Wars, Marvel, 1923, and Super Bowl halftime shows. Almost none of that work is visible. Behind every credit roll, live broadcast, and stadium tour are hundreds of craftspeople, and nearly all of that industry runs through Los Angeles and New York.

So why did she just open a storefront in Kansas City?

Recorded live, Kevin McGinnis talks with Paige about the week in New Orleans that planted the idea. She describes the day she walked out of a bridal job to join a union costume shop racing a Christmas deadline. She also recounts the back-to-back run of Beyoncé, the Super Bowl, and Lady Gaga’s Coachella, which ended with a serious talk from her mom. Now, with Cut & Sew KC open on Delaware Street, she’s making a bigger bet. She believes the Midwest already has the talent. What it’s missing is enough steady work, and enough trust, to keep that talent here.
