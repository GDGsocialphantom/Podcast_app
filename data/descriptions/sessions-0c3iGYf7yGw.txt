Kansas City's entrepreneurship ecosystem has a talent gap, a capital gap, and a connection gap. So how is the city closing them? In this Keystone Sessions talk, Adam Larson of KC SourceLink breaks down Forging the Future, a long-term initiative with the UMKC Innovation Center backed by a three-year Missouri Technology Corporation grant, designed to fund collaborative projects that strengthen Kansas City's startup ecosystem from the ground up.

Adam walks through the seven imperatives driving the program, from cultivating entrepreneurial talent to expanding access to capital, and shares real examples from the first funding cycle, including projects supporting healthcare founders, Black professionals, disabled entrepreneurs, and youth just discovering entrepreneurship as a path forward.
