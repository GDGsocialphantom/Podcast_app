Learn about LaunchKC, a nonprofit initiative co-founded by the Downtown Council and the Economic Development Corporation of Kansas City, which is dedicated to attracting and supporting high-growth tech startups in Downtown Kansas City, Missouri. This session provides an overview of LaunchKC’s efforts, including its annual investment of over $1 million through accelerators, studios, and the popular grants competition. Discover the current and future programming LaunchKC has planned to strengthen KC’s entrepreneurial ecosystem and the exciting relaunch of the grants competition.
