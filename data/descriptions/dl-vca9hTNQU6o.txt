This panel brings together diverse perspectives on the mental health challenges and wellness strategies critical to the entrepreneurial journey. Moderated by Kevin McGinnis, with insights from Ebrima "Abraham" Sisay (Freedom Project), James Glenn (University Health KC), and Dr. Alicia Johnson (AspireHealth), the conversation delves into the pressures entrepreneurs face and practical approaches for maintaining mental well-being. From stress management to resilience-building, learn how to navigate the emotional highs and lows of entrepreneurship.

Watch to learn:

Common mental health challenges faced by entrepreneurs
Practical strategies for resilience and mental wellness
Insights into balancing entrepreneurial demands with personal well-being
How to identify and manage stressors unique to the entrepreneurial lifestyle
