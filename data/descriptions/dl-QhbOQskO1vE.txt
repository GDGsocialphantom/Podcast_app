Join us for an insightful conversation in our latest edition of the Rebuild Honestly forum as we discuss 'Empowering Female Athletes: Achieving Equality in Women's Sports'. Hosted by BXKC, this panel discussion features a remarkable lineup of local sports industry leaders sharing their experiences and insights on the topic.  

 2022 was a pivotal year for women's sports as we witnessed numerous record-breaking achievements by female athletes. The release of basketball star Brittney Griner from her detention in Russia was a testament to the strength, resilience, and challenges that women in sports face. Yet, it also illuminated the clear disparities still present in the industry, forcing many professional athletes like Griner to supplement their income by playing overseas.

This panel will address the growth, impact, and importance of women's sports and highlight the work still required to create an equal playing field for all athletes. We'll explore the roadblocks and the opportunities, the triumphs, and the battles ahead.

Featuring industry leaders sharing their experiences working in this evolving field, this conversation is a must-watch for anyone passionate about the advancement of women in sports.

 Let's join hands to champion the cause for greater visibility, financial support, and infrastructure in women's sports and to open more doors for women to shine in the industry.
