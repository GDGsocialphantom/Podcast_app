In 2000, building a global company from Kansas City sounded like a punchline. Investors wouldn't back what they couldn't touch, and the city's biggest barrier wasn't talent or money. It was belief.



Neil Sharma, founder of DEG and vice chair of KC2026, built that company anyway: 650 employees, global brands, an acquisition by Dentsu. He explains why relationship capital outranks financial capital, how one anchor client changed everything, and what the World Cup actually proved: confidence is economic infrastructure. Then he lays out the next play, turning a sports moment into a sports economy, and names the regional fault line that could stall it.
