Kansas City, Kansas has been underestimated for decades. Mayor Christal Watson is done with that.

In this live taping of The Disruption Lab, host Kevin McGinnis sits down with Mayor Christal Watson  — the first Black woman to lead KCK and Wyandotte County — for an honest conversation about leading a city through fractured trust, limited resources, and cultural reinvention. From navigating the Chiefs stadium decision on day one, to rethinking how local government supports small business, to why she refuses to "always be campaigning," Mayor Watson brings a refreshing honesty rarely heard from elected officials.

In this episode:
- Why trust in institutions is fragile — and how to rebuild it
- The "imposter syndrome" holding KCK back
- Small business as infrastructure, not an afterthought
- Why local government is failing at technology adoption
- Leading without campaigning
- Regionalism and why Wyandotte County belongs at the table
- Faith, family, and the human cost of public leadership
