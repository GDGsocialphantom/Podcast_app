She trained in haute couture at the Paris American Academy: precision tailoring, centuries of tradition, the highest level of craft. Then she came home and started growing fashion out of algae and kombucha.

In this episode of The Disruption Lab, host Kevin McGinnis sits down with Nadia, founder of Biospoke, to unpack her path from classical fashion training to building biomaterials from scratch in her own kitchen. They dig into the science behind her process, the greenwashing risk facing the sustainable fashion industry, why she thinks changing how people think about their clothes matters more than telling them to stop shopping fast fashion, and the reality of running three companies: Biospoke, her workshop business, and Lumo, a "Google Maps for textile waste", as a solo founder relocating her business to a new city.

This is a conversation about craft, material science, and what it takes to build a business the industry hasn't figured out how to scale yet.
