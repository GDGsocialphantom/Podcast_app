Infrastructure without application strategy is just real estate with electricity. Sprint launched America’s first 4G network and still lost the 4G era to the translators. Having the infrastructure first is not the same as capturing the era. KC now has $11B+ of announced hyperscale investment. The economic return depends on the translation: connecting the compute to the compute-hungry industries already here. And the proximity advantage is real where it’s honest: the triangle of compute + physical testbeds + operating companies, which almost no other region has.
