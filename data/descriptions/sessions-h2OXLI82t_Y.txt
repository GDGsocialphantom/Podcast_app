Should you automate that task or protect it? Phil Ledgerwood joins Keystone Sessions to break down a practical framework for deciding what parts of your business are actually good candidates for AI automation, and which ones you should hold onto, even if a machine could technically do them better.

Phil walks through real-world examples. fFrom document acquisition and quote generation to the parts of a job that bring people genuine joy showing why "could AI do this?" is the wrong question. The real question is: should it?

Along the way, Phil unpacks a concept most leaders overlook entirely: skill degradation. Every task you hand off to a tool is a skill you slowly lose the ability to do yourself. Sometimes that's a fine trade. Sometimes it isn't. This conversation gives you a way to tell the difference.
