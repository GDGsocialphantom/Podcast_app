What if your business is just stringing actions together without moving forward?

On part two of three in this Keystone Session, Ron Green of Digistory KC (the Digital Storytelling Center of Kansas City) argues that the structure of a good story and the process of innovation are the same thing. Both need a want, a disruption, a struggle, a turning point and a transformation. He uses The Wizard of Oz to show how it works. The Wizard turns out to be a fraud, but the characters still leave changed, because what they were looking for came from facing obstacles, not from him.

Ron then tells his own story. It starts when Google Fiber came to Kansas City in 2011 and a group inside Hallmark began exploring digital storytelling. That led to the Digital Storytelling Forum with Mizzou’s journalism school, then a nonprofit that teaches stop-motion animation to kids in KC’s urban core. When the pandemic shut down in-person workshops, they built a 20-lesson online curriculum.
