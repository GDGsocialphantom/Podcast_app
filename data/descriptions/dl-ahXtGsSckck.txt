A conversation with President & CEO of Junior Achievement of Greater Kansas City, Megan Sturges Stanfield. Hear how JAKC Mentors and Volunteers inspire nearly 15,000 students from across the Kansas City metro region annually, supporting efforts to redesign the educational experience through relevant, experiential learning, supported by a continuum of progressive lessons and curriculum. JAKC’s goal is to inspire and prepare youth to demonstrate the competencies and skills needed to be financially capable, work ready, and entrepreneurially minded – ultimately curating future economic mobility and opportunities to fulfill their greatest potential.
