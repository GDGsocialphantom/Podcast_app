Join Kyle Hollins as he shares the powerful journey that inspired him to create The Lyrik's Institution, a cognitive-based program designed to empower at-risk youth through mastery in the Creative Arts. With a mission to reduce crime and violence by addressing destructive thinking patterns, The Lyrik’s Institution is changing the educational landscape by reworking negative behaviors into positive, productive actions. Discover how this innovative approach is providing a pathway to success and reshaping futures for young people in need.

Watch to learn:

The founding story of The Lyrik’s Institution and its mission
How cognitive-based learning is used to transform behavior
The impact of Creative Arts in supporting at-risk youth
Why addressing destructive thinking is key to reducing crime and violence
