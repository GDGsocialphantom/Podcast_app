Explore the story behind the Graffiti Attic at Kansas City’s Union Station, a unique space transformed into the city’s largest street art gallery. Created during the pandemic, this 11,000-square-foot area on Union Station’s seventh floor now features works from over a hundred artists. Learn how this vibrant gallery blends historic architecture with modern street art, providing a legal and inspiring venue for local artists to showcase their talent.
