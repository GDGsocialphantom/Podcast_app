In this panel on economic innovation, 2021 Venture for America Fellow Jack Conway leads a discussion with Kansas City leaders on advancing economic development through regional innovation systems, innovation districts, and clusters. Panelists Kevin McGinnis (Keystone Innovation District), Kimberly Young (Animal Health Corridor), and Dane Stangler (Bipartisan Policy Center) share insights on how these concepts are shaping Kansas City’s future and fostering an environment that supports growth and collaboration.

Watch to learn:

The role of regional innovation systems in economic development
How innovation districts drive collaboration and economic growth
The impact of industry clusters and agglomeration on Kansas City’s economy
Strategies for building a thriving, innovative community in the Midwest
