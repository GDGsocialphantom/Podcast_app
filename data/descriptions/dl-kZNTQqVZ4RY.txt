This session explores how the Kansas City community, led by the KC STEM Alliance, initiated an impactful conversation to understand and address the racial gap in their STEM workforce and education pathways. Through collaboration with educators, students, and community leaders, they identified challenges and developed a solutions-oriented approach to increase access to STEM education from PK-12. Discover the Framework for Action they created, which offers guidance for other schools, communities, and regions aiming to create inclusive STEM opportunities.

Watch to learn:

Why racial gaps exist in STEM education and workforce in Kansas City
Insights into what’s working and what’s not in addressing these gaps
How the KC STEM Alliance developed a solutions-oriented framework
Ways other communities can adopt and adapt the Framework for Action
