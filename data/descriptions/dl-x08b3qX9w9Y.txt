Join us for an exclusive fireside chat with the charismatic and innovative Gregory Stutzer, co-founder of The.WAV and Afrohaus. A talented Tanzanian American artist and producer, Gregory is revolutionizing the music scene with his unique fusion of Bongo Flava, Afrobeats, and modern pop, a genre he calls 'AfroWave'.

With features in Billboard, Fader, and Complex, and premieres on Beats1 and BBC’s 1Xtra Radio, The.WAV is making waves globally, pushing boundaries and redefining music norms. Beyond music, Gregory is also an entrepreneur, co-founding Afrohaus, a platform empowering diverse voices from the African diaspora through arts, community, and education.

In this insightful chat, Gregory shares his journey in music, entrepreneurship, and his vision for AfroWave. Explore the influences behind his groundbreaking work and his mission to amplify cultural diversity.
