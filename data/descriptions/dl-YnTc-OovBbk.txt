Join us in this enlightening presentation where Victor Hwang, founder and CEO of Right to Start, opens up about the hurdles faced by aspiring entrepreneurs in our system. Right to Start is a ground-breaking startup organization that currently operates in 28 states, fighting to expand entrepreneurial opportunity for all.

In this video, Victor discusses the organization's diligent work in policy advocacy, with a significant achievement of introducing 30 bills across 13 state legislatures this year. He provides an in-depth look into their grassroots organization strategy, highlighting the contribution of their 14 field organizers towards expanding the entrepreneurial landscape.

Furthermore, the presentation explores Right to Start's impactful media and storytelling initiatives, including op-eds and digital community engagement, designed to drive awareness and bring about positive change. Victor’s passion and commitment to empowering entrepreneurs shine through, offering viewers insight into the potential that lies in embracing entrepreneurship.

As Victor prepares to leave Kansas City, this presentation also serves as a farewell, celebrating his impactful work and wishing him well on his upcoming birthday. Whether you're an aspiring entrepreneur, a policy advocate, or someone interested in the startup ecosystem, this video provides a unique perspective on the advocacy for entrepreneurial rights.
