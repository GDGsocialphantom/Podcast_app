In this panel discussion, we explore the pivotal role that Diversity and Inclusion (D&I) play in the success and longevity of businesses in the Kansas City workforce. Despite increasing diversity, a surprising 50% of local companies do not have a comprehensive D&I policy in place. This lack of inclusion not only limits the companies' potential but also has a significant impact on employee satisfaction and retention.

What to Expect:
Insights into the current state of Diversity and Inclusion within Kansas City businesses.
Thought-provoking conversations with industry experts, thought leaders, and business professionals.
An exploration of the ROI of implementing effective D&I policies.
Real-world examples of success stories and best practices in D&I.
Discussions on how an inclusive work environment can contribute to employee retention.
