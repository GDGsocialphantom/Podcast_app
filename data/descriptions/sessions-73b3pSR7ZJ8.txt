Hashtags are dead. Instagram wants raw over polished. TikTok stopped being a viral growth machine. If your social media strategy still looks like 2024, you're leaving reach on the table.

In this Keystone Sessions talk, Gwen Gillette breaks down exactly what changed across every major platform in 2026 — and the specific moves that actually grow an account now. From Instagram's shift away from hyperlocal distribution, to TikTok's six-slide carousels, to why YouTube Shorts are now non-negotiable, this is a platform-by-platform field guide you can act on today.

What you'll learn:
- Why Instagram now rewards AI-friendly, translatable, low-text content
- The trial reels + linked reels strategy for accounts under 1,000 followers
- How TikTok's follower-first algorithm changes what you should post
- Why YouTube Shorts (and collaborations) are required for growth in 2026
- The LinkedIn carousel/document format that gets 3x more reach
- Why Facebook is quietly the best platform for local and organic growth right now
- How to think about virality, niches, and searchability across all five platforms

Whether you're a founder, content creator, or marketer, this session gives you a repeatable framework instead of chasing every algorithm update.
