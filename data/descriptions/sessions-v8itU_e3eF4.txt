If someone deposited $100,000 into your business account today, would it fix your problems or magnify them?

This is part one of three and at today's live Keystone Session, Rickey Leathers, Vice President and Senior Business Consultant at JPMorgan Chase, makes the case that capital doesn’t solve what’s broken in a business. It amplifies whatever is already there. Leathers leads Chase’s Coaching for Impact program in the Kansas City metro, giving no-cost one-on-one coaching to small business owners. In this talk he covers the cash flow and capital fundamentals that apply whether you’re launching or ten years in.
