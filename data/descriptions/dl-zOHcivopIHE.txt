There's a chemical in almost every building, car, and grocery store in America and the world just agreed to stop using it. So what happens now?

On this episode of The Disruption Lab, host Kevin McGinnis talks with Tony and Casey of EARTH, an NSF Engineering Research Center led by the University of Kansas, about the sweeping refrigerant transition already reshaping HVAC, food supply chains, and even organ transplant preservation. They break down why 90% of refrigerants leak into the atmosphere, why a 2029 production cliff is about to raise prices across the board, and why illegally smuggled refrigerant is now one of the most trafficked commodities in the U.S.

This episode explores the gap between university research and real-world commercialization, how industry consortium members like Chemours, Carrier, and Trane shape what gets funded, and where the next disruptive cooling technologies, from ultra-sensitive leak sensors to non-refrigerant solid-state cooling, might come from.
