Discover the Medici Effect and its potential to transform innovation ecosystems, particularly in regions like Kansas City. In this insightful conversation, Craig Moore (Venture For America) and Kevin McGinnis (Keystone Community Corporation) discuss the Medici Effect’s principles, which emphasize the power of cross-disciplinary collaboration to spark groundbreaking ideas. Learn how this approach has shaped their work and its impact on building a vibrant, interconnected community of innovators in Kansas City.

What the Medici Effect is and how it drives innovation
