Most founders never see the system working against them. Andrew Hsu spent 14 years inside it.

Andrew Hsu co-founded Spotlight in 2012 and spent the next 14 years helping enterprise technology companies navigate one of the most misunderstood forces in B2B markets: the industry analyst. But as AI reshapes how buyers discover, evaluate, and trust technology, everything he built his firm around is shifting — and Andrew is one of the few people who can tell you exactly how.

In this episode of The Disruption Lab, host Kevin McGinnis sits down with Andrew to unpack:
→  How industry analysts like Gartner and Forrester quietly shape which tech companies win — and why most people never see it happening
→  Why AI is forcing companies to rethink market influence from the ground up (hint: Reddit matters now)
→  The single biggest mistake founders make, even with great capital and great advisors
→  What the correlation between Glassdoor scores and stock price reveals about leadership
→  Why stepping away from a company you built is a mourning process — and what that means for how you build
→  The three dashboard signals every founder undervalues

This episode is for founders, operators, and anyone who has ever wondered who actually shapes what the market believes — and whether that system is about to change forever.
