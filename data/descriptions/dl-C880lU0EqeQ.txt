Empower your career growth with actionable strategies from industry-leading experts! As part of the Black Excellence Inc's Rebuild Honestly Panel Series, we present a special Juneteenth event centered on addressing representation fatigue in the corporate world.

What is Representation Fatigue? It's a challenge frequently encountered by Black professionals, symbolizing the exhaustion faced due to continuous efforts to represent and educate in predominantly non-diverse environments. In this enlightening discussion, our panelists - Dr. Aishah Augusta-Parham, Dr. Ashli Owens, and Abdul Yahaya, offer their profound insights rooted in years of experience in workplace diversity, equity, and inclusion.

Hosted by the dynamic Lexi Holifield, known for her relentless advocacy for advancement and community growth, this event promises to arm you with knowledge and techniques to navigate, overcome, and thrive amidst representation fatigue. Whether you're looking to understand the issue or seeking solutions for personal growth, this discussion is your guide to flourishing in the corporate space.
