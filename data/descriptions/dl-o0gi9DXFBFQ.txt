Brandon survived 12 strokes. Lindsay has been his care partner for 15 years. Now they're co-founders of Beyond This, building the relationship and intimacy support system the healthcare industry left out.

In this episode of the Social Venture Studio Founder series, host Kevin McGinnis sits down with Brandon and Lindsay to unpack what happens after the hospital sends you home: the identity loss, the isolation, the five years it took to hug his own wife again, and the data behind it. 85% of people leaving therapy want help with relationships or sexual health, but only 5% get it from a therapist, and 60–80% of relationships don't survive a disability.

They talk about building Beyond This from a rehab facility hallway to a real company in under 90 days, co-founding as spouses, the "Levels of Spice" framework, and what it means to build a business that finds you instead of the other way around.
