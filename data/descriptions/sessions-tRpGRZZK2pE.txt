What happens when entrepreneurs from Trinidad, Mexico, and the Dominican Republic bring their business instincts to Kansas City — and what do they bring back home?

In this Keystone Sessions conversation, Global Ties KC fellows share the real, unfiltered story of building businesses across borders: a curriculum specialist reshaping literacy programs, a founder who built a data tool from scratch to help adults with disabilities access employment services, and an avocado exporter navigating weather, brokers, and supply chains no one controls.

They talk about what makes Kansas City's business culture different — the networking, the follow-up meetings, the volunteer spirit — and what KC organizations could learn from how small businesses in Latin America and the Caribbean cut costs without cutting quality. And they get real about the leap into entrepreneurship: the doubt, the isolation, the family members who didn't believe in them, and the moment it finally clicked.

This is a conversation about global business, cross-cultural leadership, and what it actually takes to build something meaningful — no matter where you start.
