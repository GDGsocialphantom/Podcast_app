Kansas City isn't just a logistics hub, it's home to the only single-line railroad connecting Canada and Mexico, 5 Class I railroad networks, and a foreign trade zone system most Americans have never heard of. Chris Gutierrez, President of KC SmartPort, breaks down how Kansas City became a supply chain powerhouse and where automation, robotics, and AI data centers are about to create the next generation of jobs in the region.

This is a story about infrastructure, yes. But it's really a story about what gets built when a region stops waiting to be noticed.
