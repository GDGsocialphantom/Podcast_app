Most people think growing on social media is about posting more content, more often. Gwen Gillette says that's backwards and in this Keystone Sessions conversation, she breaks down the content engagement strategy that actually moves the needle in 2026.

Gwen walks through why outbound engagement can grow an account faster than content alone, how to build a platform-specific funnel that turns followers into customers, and why AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is becoming just as important as SEO now that AI tools are driving a growing share of searches. She also unpacks viral modeling — a repeatable method for finding what's actually working in your niche before you build content around it.

Whether you're a founder, marketer, or small business owner trying to make sense of an increasingly fragmented content landscape, this episode gives you a practical system, not just theory.
