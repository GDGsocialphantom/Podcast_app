In 2013, Kevin McGinnis helped launch a Hack Friday program inside Sprint — full-day hackathons where cross-functional teams tackled real business problems. Participation grew every month. Then corporate leadership shut it down, because it hadn't shipped a single solution yet.

In this solo episode, Kevin unpacks why that moment wasn't a one-off failure — it's the structural flaw baked into how most corporations approach innovation. Drawing on sociologist Ronald Burt's research on network bridging, Frans Johansson's "Medici Effect," and the economic theory of absorptive capacity, Kevin lays out why proximity to an innovation ecosystem — not a defined ROI — is the real investment. He closes with four concrete moves leaders can make to protect the parts of innovation that don't show up on a quarterly dashboard.

If you lead a team, sit inside a corporate innovation function, or have ever watched a promising initiative get killed for not "shipping" fast enough, this one's for you.
