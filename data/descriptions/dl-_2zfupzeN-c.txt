Is nuclear power finally having its breakthrough — or is this another false start? In this live episode of The Disruption Lab, host Kevin McGinnis talks with Jason Pottorff of Deep Fission and Dr. Amir Bahadori of Kansas State University about the real forces driving the nuclear renaissance: surging AI and data center electricity demand, a massive workforce shortage, advanced small reactor designs, and the public-trust problem that has held the industry back for decades.
Jason breaks down Deep Fission's "Gravity Reactor" — a small reactor buried a mile underground in a borehole — and why it could make nuclear cheaper and safer. Amir explains how K-State rebuilt its nuclear engineering program and why radiation risk communication may be the toughest challenge in the field. Together, they map out the emerging Kansas–Missouri "nuclear corridor" and what U.S. energy could look like by 2031 and 2050.
