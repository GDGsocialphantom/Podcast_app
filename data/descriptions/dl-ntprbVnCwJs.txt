Brian spent decades in advertising building campaigns that became part of American culture; Sonic's "two guys," Southwest's "Now Free to Move About the Country", before doing something most agency creatives never do: becoming the client. Eleven years into leading Garmin's in-house creative organization, he's outlasted his own rule that clients get tired of you after ten years.

In this episode, Brian Brooker breaks down what it really takes to build a 110-person creative team inside an engineering-led company, why "going all in" is the only version of in-housing that works, how he pushes for authenticity against the temptation of AI shortcuts, and the lessons from 40 years in the business about why simplicity beats cleverness every time.
