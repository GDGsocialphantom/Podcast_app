Half of the people experiencing homelessness in America right now have jobs. So why aren't we talking about that?

In this episode of The Disruption Lab, host Kevin McGinnis sits down with Jarrod Sanderson, Executive Director of The Way Home, to challenge the story we've been telling about affordable housing — and why the way we fund, frame, and coordinate around this issue is the real bottleneck.

Jared spent 18 years in social work before taking a detour into light industrial logistics — where he discovered that the #1 reason he was losing dock workers was unstable housing. There was no escaping the problem. So he stopped running from it.

In this conversation, you'll hear why:
— Affordable housing needs to compete at market speed, not charity speed
— Data fragmentation is quietly killing coordination across the housing continuum
— Mixed-income portfolios (not projects) may be the fastest path to scale
— The "need vs. deserve" debate is a gateway to doing nothing
— Kansas City has the talent, the capital, and the capacity to solve this — if the system gets out of its own way

This isn't a conversation about sympathy. It's a conversation about infrastructure, capital stacks, systems design, and what real disruption looks like when the stakes are people's lives.
