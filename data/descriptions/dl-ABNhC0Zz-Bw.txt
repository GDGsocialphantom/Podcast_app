In the kickoff to our Sustainable Infrastructure series, join Kevin McGinnis as he hosts leaders from Climate Action KC, the Mid-America Regional Council, and The Building Energy Exchange KC to discuss the pressing impacts of climate change and the urgent need for sustainable solutions. This panel explores the importance of innovation in building resilient infrastructure, reducing carbon footprints, and addressing environmental challenges that impact Kansas City and beyond.

Watch to learn:

The current impacts of climate change on infrastructure and communities
Why sustainable infrastructure is critical for resilience and growth
Innovative solutions and strategies from Kansas City’s leaders
How regional collaboration can drive climate action and sustainability
