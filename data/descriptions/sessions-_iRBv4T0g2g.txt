Financial confidence isn't about knowing everything, it's about understanding a few key concepts that change how you make decisions. In this Keystone Sessions talk, Miriah breaks down the financial fundamentals most people never get taught, with a particular focus on the barriers facing foster youth navigating money and credit for the first time.

From why saving $5 a week matters more than the amount, to how compound interest quietly builds wealth (or debt), to the predatory lending traps designed to look like relief, this is a practical, judgment-free walkthrough of the financial system and how to work within it.
