Somewhere tonight, a family is getting the phone call. Tomorrow they’ll drive to Kansas City. Where do they sleep?

On this episode of The Disruption Lab, recorded live in front of a studio audience, host Kevin McGinnis sits down with Tami Greenberg, CEO of Ronald McDonald House Kansas City. Her organization serves about 5,000 families a year and still has to say no to hundreds more. Tami explains why the healthcare system makes room for the sick child but not the parent, and why research shows kids get better faster when their families are cared for. She also explains how a social worker with no political experience ended up leading a successful public vote to buy the city park her house was built on.
