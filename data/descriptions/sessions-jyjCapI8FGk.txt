You’d never hand a stranger $100 without asking how and when you’d get it back. So why do so many business owners walk into a bank without answering that same question?

This is part two of three of this Keystone Session and today Rickey Leathers breaks down what loan readiness really means, drawing on years of coaching hundreds of small business owners through the lending process. Most of those owners describe getting a bank loan the same way: hurdles and hoops. Rickey argues the process makes more sense once you see it from the lender’s side. The single most common mistake he sees is a business owner who builds detailed projections for growth and forgets to include the loan payment itself.
