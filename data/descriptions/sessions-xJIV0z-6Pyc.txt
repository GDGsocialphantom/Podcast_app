Every bank has a long menu of products. For credit, though, Rickey Leathers says there are only three moves. Change the name, add a feature, and it’s still a credit card, a line of credit, or a term loan. The trick is knowing which one fits what you’re paying for.

On the last part of this Keystone Session, Rickey breaks down each of those three options and when to use them. He explains how the SBA and community lenders make capital easier to reach for small businesses. In an open Q&A, he takes on the questions owners actually bring him: business credit “hacks,” using an SBA 7(a) loan to buy an existing company, and whether a grant can count toward your down payment.
