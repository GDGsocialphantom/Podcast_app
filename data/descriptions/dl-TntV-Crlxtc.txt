For 25 years, Dick Jobe was Kansas City’s “Dr. Volvo,” the mechanic the city trusted with its cars. Then he walked away from the top of one trade to start over in another. Today his work hangs in airports, hotels, and NASCAR’s Victory Lane, and almost none of it carries his name. Recorded live at Keystone Sessions, Kevin McGinnis talks with the founder of Machine Head about the craft between an artist’s sketch and a finished install: wind load, seismic cables, a freight elevator nobody measured. Then the harder questions: what happens to a city’s public art when no one local can build it, and who learns the trade when the founder steps back?
