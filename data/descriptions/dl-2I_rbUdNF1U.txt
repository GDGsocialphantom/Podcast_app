Courtney presides over nearly every domestic violence case filed in Kansas City Municipal Court which is around 200 a day. Thaddeus spent his career building data systems at Amazon, Cerner, and CVS Health. In this episode of The Disruption Lab, part of the Social Venture Studio Founder Series, host Kevin McGinnis explores how a sitting judge and a data scientist built a company together and what it actually takes to sell technology into one of the most risk-averse systems in the country.
