Free guides don't convert anymore. Word-based hooks are dead. And your business account might be killing your reach. In this episode of Keystone Sessions, Gwen Gillette breaks down the exact shifts happening across social media and content marketing in 2026 and what businesses need to do differently to actually reach consumers.
Gwen unpacks why visual hooks have replaced scripted openers, why loopable content is winning the algorithm game, and how answer engine optimization (AEO) is changing the way people discover businesses through AI tools like ChatGPT. She also explains the death of the free lead magnet, why low-cost paid offers now outperform them, and how to choose the right platform based on your audience, offer, and goals not just what's trending.
Whether you're a founder, a creator, or running marketing for a growing business, this conversation gives you a practical framework for building trust, growing an email list, and creating content that actually drives revenue in 2026.
