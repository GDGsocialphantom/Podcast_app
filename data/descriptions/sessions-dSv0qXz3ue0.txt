What can you say to a whole country when you don’t speak its language?

When Juniper Tangpuz was invited to Bulgaria, he found a pile of trash on the street. He turned it into a cardboard dog with bottle-cap eyes, then walked it around town to connect with people he couldn’t talk to. That’s how he works: he finds the material nobody wanted and puts the art where art usually isn’t.

On part 1 of 2 of this Keystone Sessions, the Kansas City artist and University of Kansas School of Architecture & Design staff member traces his path from wanting to be a cartoonist, to making pop-up books, to building sculpture and public installations. He shows paper instruments that actually play, giant festival creatures made of corrugated plastic, and installations that invite people to finish the story themselves.
