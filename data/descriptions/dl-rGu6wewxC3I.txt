Montré Moore, co-founder of AMP Beauty, spent years inside prestige retail at Neiman Marcus and Revolve, managing a $14 million cosmetics department and watching underrepresented brand founders get locked out by red tape and gatekeeping. Then one night in downtown LA, unable to find a basic beauty product within 45 minutes, she saw the whole system's failure firsthand. That moment became AMP Beauty, a platform now working to democratize retail access for emerging founders through data, technology, and a proprietary Retail Readiness Score.
