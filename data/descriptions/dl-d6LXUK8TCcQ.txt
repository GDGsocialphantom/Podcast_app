In this Black Excellence KC panel, leaders in community development and real estate from the Kansas City Area Development Council, Crossroads Real Estate Group, and Keystone Innovation District discuss the challenges and opportunities within Kansas City’s development efforts. Moderated by Craig Moore, Founder of Black Excellence KC, this session explores the impact of current projects on the Black community and the initiatives underway to create greater access and economic opportunity for all.

Watch to learn:

The growing pains and successes in Kansas City’s development landscape
How current projects are impacting the Black community
Efforts by key organizations to create inclusive access and opportunity
Insights on fostering equitable community growth in real estate
This session is essential for anyone interested in community development, real estate, and building a more inclusive Kansas City. Don’t forget to like, share, and subscribe for more conversations on community impact and economic opportunity!
