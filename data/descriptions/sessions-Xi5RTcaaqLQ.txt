Gamers are the most ad-resistant, community-driven audience on the planet which is exactly why they became the proving ground for the future of marketing. In this Keystone Sessions talk, the co-founder of the Kansas City Pioneers esports organization and Social Apex Media, breaks down the 5-step playbook brands use to earn genuine attention in a space where interruption is rejected outright — and why that same playbook works in any industry.

From nearly a decade of trial and error building multiple businesses simultaneously, our guest unpacks how gaming quietly proved integrated marketing at scale before other industries caught on, and what founders and marketers can steal from it today.
