Join a panel of Kansas City’s top craft brewers as they share their personal journeys into the brewing industry and discuss what drives their passion for innovation. Located in the heart of Kansas City's Brewer's Alley along 18th Street, panelists Eric Martens (Border Brewing), Joe Giammanco (City Barrel), and Rafi Chaudry (Torn Label Brewing Co.) provide an insider’s look into the craft brewery scene. Discover the challenges, inspirations, and creative processes that keep them brewing some of Kansas City’s finest beers.

Watch to learn:

The unique paths each brewer took into the craft beer industry
What inspires Kansas City brewers to keep innovating and experimenting
Insights into the challenges and rewards of the local craft beer scene
How Brewer’s Alley fosters a community for creativity and collaboration
This conversation is perfect for craft beer enthusiasts, aspiring brewers, and anyone interested in the culture of Kansas City’s brewing industry. Don’t forget to like, share, and subscribe for more insights into local business innovation!
