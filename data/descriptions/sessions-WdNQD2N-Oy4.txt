How do you adapt an age old system to continue to keep up with modern day tech?

On part 3 of 3 of this Keystone Sessions takeover, the JE Dunn leaders explain how virtual design and construction (VDC) starts and ends with detailed modeling to resolve rebar and embed congestion, generate survey-grade layout points (northing/easting/elevation), and guide field crews using robotic total stations and GPS. 

They describe using site-printing robots to mark tens of thousands of slab anchor-bolt locations for a data center, laser scanning and SLAM capture to verify as-builts and resolve disputes, and tight-tolerance custom formwork and mockups for projects like the Kansas City Zoo aquarium. They highlight Trimble/Autodesk cloud platforms for sharing models, quantities, approvals, and discrepancies; augmented reality with SiteVision for on-site visualization and pre-pour verification; machine control for safer, lower-waste excavation; and Microsoft Copilot for secure AI workflows and meeting minutes. Evan also covers design generative studies, AI renderings, the Lens estimating database, Form prefabrication, and examples like Three Light and the Teddy Roosevelt Presidential Library, plus discussion of access, permissions, security, internships, and a top-down tech vetting process.
