Victor Hwang is back — The Disruption Lab's first returning guest, in its first virtual episode. Last time, the founder and CEO of Right to Start made the argument: entrepreneurship is a civic priority. This time he brings the scorecard. Right to Start's 2026 Missouri Entrepreneurship Profile shows a state that beats three-quarters of America at starting businesses — and almost nobody at keeping them alive at years one and five. Victor and Kevin McGinnis walk the funnel, find the leak ("entrepreneurs die deaths of a thousand cuts"), and follow the surprise in the policy data: taxes score fine, education scores best — the basement is child care, health care, red tape, and an unemployment system that won't let founders found. Plus: what a legislator, a mayor, and an ecosystem leader should each do Monday morning.
