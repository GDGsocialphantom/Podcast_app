In this BX Rebuild Honestly session, leaders from Lyrik’s Institution, aSTEAM Village, and the Urban League of Greater Kansas City discuss the critical youth programs they lead to support Black youth in Kansas City. Learn about the innovative initiatives designed to address unique challenges, break down barriers, and create long-term community impact. This panel explores the goals, obstacles, and successes of these organizations in fostering positive change and opportunity for the next generation.

Watch to learn:

How non-profits are addressing the needs of Black youth in KCMO
Key barriers Black youth face and strategies to overcome them
The goals and long-term vision for community impact in Kansas City
How youth programs are making a difference in local communities
